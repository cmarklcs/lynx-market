Print Label Cards

For teachers: prints every product as a fold-over card, 4 cards per page. Cut along the solid lines, then fold on the dashed line so the package front and its Nutrition Facts label end up back to back.

Tip: in the print dialog, turn on “Background graphics” so the package colors print.

Lynx Market is a pretend store for a 7th grade science project. Every brand, product, price and number here is made up.
